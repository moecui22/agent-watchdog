# Research briefing

## 1. Model–cortex alignment in face perception (bioRxiv)
Read in full. Trains a CNN on identity and compares its layers with fMRI responses in face-selective cortex; mid layers align best with OFA, late layers with FFA. Relevance: high — same stimulus set as our face-identification task.

## 2. Webcam eye tracking for remote cognitive screening (PsyArXiv)
Read in full. 412 participants; calibration error 1.9° median; test-retest r = .71 for saccade latency. Relevance: medium.
